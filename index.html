RULES & CARD GUIDE

WIN CONDITION: Be the first player to clear all visible/untreated Disorders from your Psyche!

Core Gameplay:

  • Turn Flow: 2 cards are drawn automatically at the start of your turn (does not cost actions). You have 2 actions per turn to play cards or draw extra cards.
  • Hand Limit: At the end of your turn, you must have 6 or fewer cards in hand.
  • Quick Fix Vulnerabilities: Playing a Quick Fix (Drug) shields your Disorder, but exposes specific vulnerable side-effect crises that opponents can play directly on top of!
  • Therapy / Safe Zone: Playing an Actual Miracle (Therapy) on an untreated Disorder purges the stack and permanently locks that slot into a CURED Safe Zone.
  • Meltdown (Black): Play temporarily on any untreated Disorder across any player to trigger its penalty punishment, then discard it.

Card Lexicon & Guide:

Dark Crisis (White Card): Psychological conditions that start in your Psyche slots. Must be treated or purged to win.
Quick Fix / Drug (White Card): Played on matching Disorders to shield them. Exposes side-effect vulnerabilities for opponents to exploit.
Actual Miracle / Therapy (White Card): Purges a disorder stack entirely and turns the slot into an unassailable Cured Safe Zone.
Total Meltdown (Black Card): Played temporarily on any untreated crisis to instantly trigger its devastating punishment.

VICTORY!

You successfully cleared your Psyche of all dark crises!
